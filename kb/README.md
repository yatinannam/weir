# Weir General Hospital knowledge base

Weir General Hospital, 12 Riverside Road, Weir City, is a fictional 400-bed teaching hospital invented for a portfolio project. It has three buildings: the Main Block (Ground to 6th floor), the OPD Block (Ground to 3rd floor) and the Mother and Child Wing (Ground to 3rd floor). All facts, prices and extensions are invented. Contact points are 4-digit internal extensions only. `kb/public/` holds 30 patient-facing documents and `kb/staff/` holds 10 staff-only documents.

## Planted look-alike pairs

1. General ward vs ICU visiting hours
   - pub-visiting-hours-general: Monday to Friday 4 pm to 7 pm; Saturday, Sunday and public holidays 11 am to 1 pm and 4 pm to 7 pm; two visitors at a time.
   - pub-visiting-hours-icu: every day 11 am to 11:30 am and 5 pm to 6 pm; one visitor at a time; same on weekends.
2. Cardiology vs Neurology OPD
   - pub-opd-cardiology: Monday, Wednesday, Friday, 9 am to 1 pm; Room 112; ext. 2140; first consultation ₹600.
   - pub-opd-neurology: Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday, 10 am to 2 pm; Room 118; ext. 2150; first consultation ₹700.
3. Car vs two-wheeler parking, weekday vs weekend
   - pub-parking, car: weekdays ₹40 first 2 hours then ₹20 per hour, max ₹200; weekends and holidays ₹60 first 2 hours then ₹30 per hour, max ₹300.
   - pub-parking, two-wheeler: weekdays ₹10 first 2 hours then ₹5 per hour, max ₹50; weekends and holidays ₹15 first 2 hours then ₹8 per hour, max ₹70.
4. Adult vs child vaccination clinic
   - pub-vaccination-clinic, adult: Tuesday and Thursday, 10 am to 1 pm, Room G4.
   - pub-vaccination-clinic, child: Monday, Wednesday and Friday, 9 am to 12 noon, Room G6. Closed on weekends for both.
5. Nurse vs doctor shift timings
   - staff-shift-timings, nurses: 7 am to 2 pm, 2 pm to 9 pm, 9 pm to 7 am.
   - staff-shift-timings, doctors: 8 am to 4 pm, 4 pm to 12 midnight, 12 midnight to 8 am.
6. Cashless insurance vs self-pay admission deposit
   - pub-billing-insurance: ₹5,000 deposit.
   - pub-billing-self-pay: ₹25,000 deposit. (Government scheme: no deposit, pub-billing-government-scheme.)
7. Private vs semi-private room tariff
   - pub-room-tariffs, private: ₹6,000 per day, 6th floor, single bed, own bathroom.
   - pub-room-tariffs, semi-private: ₹3,500 per day, 5th floor, 2 beds, shared bathroom. (General ward ₹1,200; ICU ₹9,000.)
8. Main pharmacy vs OPD pharmacy
   - pub-pharmacy, Main pharmacy: open 24 hours every day, Main Block ground floor, ext. 2115.
   - pub-pharmacy, OPD pharmacy: Monday to Saturday 8 am to 6 pm, closed Sunday, OPD Block ground floor, ext. 2116.
