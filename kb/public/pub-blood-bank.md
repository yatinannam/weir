## Location and hours
The blood bank is on the 1st floor of the Main Block, ext. 2230. Issue of blood to patients is available 24 hours a day. Voluntary donation is accepted Monday to Saturday from 9 am to 5 pm.

## Getting blood for a patient
1. The treating doctor writes a blood requisition form.
2. A relative brings the form, the patient's admission number and a photo ID to the blood bank counter.
3. A sample of the patient's blood is sent for cross-matching.
4. Pay the processing fee and collect the blood bag.

Processing fees per unit are ₹1,200 for whole blood and ₹400 for plasma units. Fees are waived for patients under the government scheme.

## Replacement donors
Families are encouraged, but not required, to arrange one replacement donor for each unit issued. If no donor is available, blood is still issued in an emergency.

## Becoming a donor
- Age between 18 and 60 years.
- Body weight of at least 50 kg.
- Bring a photo ID.
- Donation takes about 30 minutes in total, including a short rest and a snack.
- A donor can donate again after 3 months.

## Camps
The blood bank holds a donation camp on the first Saturday of every month in the Main Block lobby, from 10 am to 3 pm. Donors receive a certificate and a free health checkup coupon worth ₹500.

## Contact
Call ext. 2230 between 8 am and 8 pm for stock enquiries. A relative must visit the counter for issue.
