## Location and hours
The public cafeteria is on the ground floor of the Main Block, east wing, near Gate 1. It is open every day from 7 am to 10 pm. A small tea and snack kiosk in the OPD Block ground floor is open Monday to Saturday from 8 am to 5 pm.

## Meal timings
- Breakfast: 7 am to 10:30 am
- Lunch: 12 noon to 3 pm
- Evening snacks: 4 pm to 7 pm
- Dinner: 7 pm to 10 pm

Tea, coffee and packaged snacks are available throughout opening hours.

## Prices
- Vegetarian thali: ₹120
- Breakfast combo (two items and tea): ₹70
- Tea or coffee: ₹15
- Packaged water bottle (1 litre): ₹20
- Fruit plate: ₹60

Payment is by cash, card or UPI at the billing counter. Staff members with a hospital ID badge pay at the staff rate, which is 20 percent lower.

## Patient meals
Meals for inpatients are included in the room tariff and delivered to the bed. Attendants can buy meal coupons at the cafeteria counter. A coupon for one meal costs ₹100 and is valid for 7 days.

## Seating and rules
- Seating for 120 people, with a family section.
- Take-away boxes cost ₹5 each.
- Patients on special meal plans should order only from their ward menu, not from the cafeteria.
- Smoking and alcohol are not allowed anywhere on campus.
