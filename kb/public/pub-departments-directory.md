## Buildings
Weir General Hospital has three buildings: the Main Block (Ground to 6th floor), the OPD Block (Ground to 3rd floor) and the Mother and Child Wing (Ground to 3rd floor). All are connected by a covered walkway on the ground floor.

## Tips
Dial the extension from any hospital phone. From outside, call the main reception and ask to be connected. Lifts in the Main Block are on the north side; the OPD Block has a ramp at the front entrance.
