## Visiting windows
The Intensive Care Unit (ICU) is on the 3rd floor of the Main Block. Visiting is allowed twice a day, every day of the week including weekends and public holidays:

- Morning: 11 am to 11:30 am
- Evening: 5 pm to 6 pm

There is no separate weekend schedule. The windows are the same on all seven days.

## Visitor limits
Only one visitor is allowed inside the ICU at a time, and each visit is limited to the window above. Only immediate family members listed on the patient's admission form may enter. Children under 16 are not permitted.

## Entry steps
1. Collect an ICU visitor pass from the 3rd floor waiting area desk. Bring a photo ID.
2. Wash or sanitise hands at the entry station.
3. Wear the shoe covers and gown provided by the ICU staff.
4. Leave bags, phones and food in the lockers outside. Lockers are free.

## Updates for families
Doctors speak to one nominated family member daily at 12:30 pm in the ICU family room. The nominee is recorded at admission and can be changed by speaking to the ICU nurse in charge.

## Exceptions
Extra visits outside the windows can be approved only by the ICU consultant on duty, for example for a family member arriving from out of town. Ask the ICU desk on ext. 2130.

These hours are different from the general ward timings, which are longer and allow two visitors.
