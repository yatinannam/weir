## Your rights
Every patient at Weir General Hospital has the right to:
- Be treated with dignity and respect, regardless of age, gender, religion or ability to pay.
- Receive emergency care without any deposit.
- Know the name and role of every doctor and nurse involved in their care.
- Receive an itemised estimate before a planned admission, within 4 hours of asking at the Admission desk (ext. 2105).
- Get a clear explanation of the procedure in a language they understand, and give written consent before any procedure.
- Refuse a procedure, and request a second opinion at the hospital's cost of consultation.
- Privacy during examinations and confidentiality of records.
- Receive copies of their records on application. See the medical records document.
- Make a complaint without fear of any effect on their care.

## Your responsibilities
- Give correct and complete personal and contact details.
- Follow the hospital rules on visiting hours, silence and no-smoking.
- Respect staff and other patients. Abusive behaviour towards staff can result in a visitor being asked to leave, and security on ext. 2400 may be called.
- Pay bills on time, as per the admission agreement.
- Keep personal valuables safe.

## Interpreters and special needs
Interpreters for sign language and six regional languages are available on 24 hours' notice; ask at Patient relations, ext. 2120. A wheelchair, ramp access and a helper are available free at every entrance.

## Posting of rights
A copy of this charter is displayed at the reception, in each ward and at the Emergency desk. A printed copy can be collected from Patient relations, Monday to Saturday, 9 am to 5 pm.
