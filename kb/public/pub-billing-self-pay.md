## Admission deposit
Patients paying from their own funds pay a deposit of ₹25,000 at the Billing counter when they are admitted for a planned stay. The deposit is adjusted against the final bill. This is higher than the deposit for cashless insurance patients, which is ₹5,000.

## Billing counters
The Billing department is on the ground floor of the Main Block, ext. 2110. Counters are open daily from 8 am to 8 pm. After 8 pm, emergency billing is handled at the Emergency desk.

## Payment modes
Cash, debit and credit cards, UPI and bank transfers are accepted. Cheques are not accepted. Cash payments above ₹2,00,000 are not accepted by law; use card or bank transfer.

## Interim bills
- An interim bill is issued every 3 days.
- If the running bill crosses the deposit, you will be asked to top up. The top-up amount is ₹10,000 or the estimate for the next 3 days, whichever is higher.
- Pay interim bills at the counter and keep every receipt.

## Final settlement
1. The final bill is ready within 2 hours of the discharge order.
2. Pay the balance, or collect a refund of any unused deposit.
3. Refunds are described in the refunds document.

## Package rates
Many planned surgeries have fixed package rates, which include room, procedure and standard consumables. Ask the Admission desk (ext. 2105) for a written estimate before admission.

## Bill queries
Bring the bill to the Billing counter or call ext. 2110. Queries are answered within one working day.
