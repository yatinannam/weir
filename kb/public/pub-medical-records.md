## Location and hours
The Medical Records department is on the 2nd floor of the OPD Block, ext. 2210. It is open Monday to Friday from 9 am to 4 pm and on Saturdays from 9 am to 12 noon. It is closed on Sundays and public holidays.

## What you can request
- Discharge summary copy
- Copies of inpatient case sheets and investigation reports
- Copies of OPD records
- Birth and death certificates issued by the hospital
- A fitness or medical certificate (needs the consulting doctor's signature)

## Who can apply
The patient with a photo ID, or an authorised person with a signed authorisation letter, the patient's photo ID copy and their own photo ID. For a deceased patient, the next of kin applies with the death certificate. For a patient under 18, a parent applies.

## Steps
1. Fill in the records request form at the counter.
2. Attach the documents listed above.
3. Pay the fee.
4. Collect the copies on the date given on the receipt.

## Fees and timing
- First copy of a discharge summary: ₹50. Each additional copy: ₹20.
- Case sheet copies: ₹5 per page, with a minimum of ₹100.
- Standard processing time: 3 working days. Urgent processing in 1 working day costs ₹200 extra.

## Storage
Inpatient records are kept for 10 years and OPD records for 5 years. Records are released only to authorised people to protect patient privacy.

## Queries
Call ext. 2210 between 9 am and 4 pm.
