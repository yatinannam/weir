---

## Location and hours
The parking area is reached through Gate 2 on Canal Lane. It is open 24 hours and holds 150 cars and 300 two-wheelers. The parking office is on ext. 2410.

## Car parking rates
- Weekdays (Monday to Friday): ₹40 for the first 2 hours, then ₹20 for each additional hour. Daily maximum ₹200.
- Weekends and public holidays (Saturday and Sunday): ₹60 for the first 2 hours, then ₹30 for each additional hour. Daily maximum ₹300.

## Two-wheeler parking rates
- Weekdays: ₹10 for the first 2 hours, then ₹5 for each additional hour. Daily maximum ₹50.
- Weekends and public holidays: ₹15 for the first 2 hours, then ₹8 for each additional hour. Daily maximum ₹70.

Two-wheeler rates are much lower than car rates on every day of the week.

## Passes and concessions
- Monthly pass: ₹1,200 for cars and ₹300 for two-wheelers. Apply at the parking office with the vehicle registration certificate.
- Inpatient attendants: 50 percent off the daily maximum on showing a valid attendant pass.
- Ambulances and patients arriving on stretchers: free for the first 30 minutes at Gate 3 drop-off.
- Doctors and staff with a valid hospital ID badge park free in the staff area.

## Rules
- Pay at the exit booth by cash, card or UPI. A lost ticket costs ₹100 extra.
- Parking is at the owner's risk. Do not leave valuables in the vehicle.
- No overnight parking without a pass, except for the vehicles of admitted patients' attendants.
