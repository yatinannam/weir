## Days and times
The Cardiology outpatient clinic runs on Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays from 9 am to 1 pm. It is closed on Tuesdays, Thursdays, Saturdays and Sundays.

## Location
OPD Block, 1st floor, Room 112. The Cardiology OPD desk is on ext. 2140. The clinic is next door to the Neurology OPD, which runs on different days.

## Fees
- First consultation: ₹600
- Follow-up within 15 days with the same doctor: ₹300
- ECG and other tests are billed separately at the OPD Block ground floor counter.

## Booking
1. Walk in and register at the OPD Block ground floor registration counter between 8 am and 12:30 pm on a clinic day.
2. Or book a slot by calling ext. 2140 between 9 am and 4 pm on any working day.
3. Collect a token. Tokens are given in order of arrival, with booked slots called first.

A maximum of 60 tokens are issued per clinic day.

## What to bring
- Photo ID
- Previous prescriptions and reports
- Insurance card, if you plan to use cashless benefits for any later admission
- A referral letter, if you have one (not mandatory)

## Good to know
Please arrive 20 minutes before your slot. Senior citizens above 65 years have a separate queue. For heart-related emergencies do not wait for the OPD; go straight to the Emergency department on the ground floor of the Main Block.
