## Location and hours
The laboratory is on the ground floor of the OPD Block, ext. 2195. Sample collection is open Monday to Saturday from 7 am to 6 pm and on Sundays from 7 am to 12 noon. The laboratory processes emergency samples 24 hours a day.

## Collecting a sample
1. Take the doctor's test request slip to the lab registration counter.
2. Pay the test charges at the counter. Insurance patients can use the OPD billing counter.
3. Collect your sample token. Fasting samples are taken between 7 am and 10 am only.
4. Provide the sample at the collection room.

## When reports are ready
- Routine blood and urine tests: same day by 6 pm if the sample is given before 11 am, otherwise the next morning.
- Cultures and specialised tests: 3 to 5 working days.
- Reports for admitted patients are sent to the ward directly.

## How to get your report
- Collect at the report counter between 8 am and 8 pm with your token and photo ID.
- Download from the patient portal using the token number and mobile number given at registration.
- A relative may collect a report with an authorisation letter and their own photo ID.

## Charges
- Duplicate printed copy of a report: ₹50
- Home sample collection within 8 km: ₹150 plus test charges, booked a day ahead on ext. 2195.

## Reports kept
Lab reports are stored for 5 years. For older records, contact Medical Records on ext. 2210.
