## Days and times
The Orthopedics outpatient clinic runs Monday to Saturday from 9 am to 12 noon. A separate plaster and dressing room is open from 9 am to 4 pm on the same days. The clinic is closed on Sundays and public holidays.

## Location
OPD Block, 2nd floor, Room 205. The Orthopedics OPD desk is on ext. 2160. The plaster room is next to the clinic. X-rays are taken at Radiology on the 1st floor of the Main Block (ext. 2190).

## Fees
- First consultation: ₹500
- Follow-up within 15 days: ₹250
- Plaster room charges are given at the counter before the procedure.

## Booking
1. Walk in and register at the OPD Block ground floor counter between 8 am and 11:30 am.
2. Or call ext. 2160 between 9 am and 4 pm to reserve a slot.
3. Up to 80 tokens are issued per day.

## What to bring
- Photo ID
- Earlier X-ray films, scan CDs and reports
- Any joint or fracture records from other hospitals
- Comfortable loose clothing that can be adjusted easily

## Good to know
Wheelchairs and walking-frame loans are available at the OPD Block entrance on submission of a photo ID, which is returned when the equipment is brought back. A refundable deposit of ₹500 applies for crutches. Patients arriving after an injury at night should go to the Emergency department.
