## Location and hours
Radiology is on the 1st floor of the Main Block, ext. 2190. The department is open every day from 8 am to 8 pm for X-rays and ultrasound. CT and MRI are scheduled from 8 am to 6 pm, Monday to Saturday. Emergency scans are done 24 hours a day.

## Services without appointment
Plain X-rays are done on a walk-in basis. Bring the doctor's request. The typical wait is 30 minutes and the report is ready within 4 hours.

## Services that need an appointment
- Ultrasound: book at the counter or on ext. 2190. Charge ₹900.
- CT scan: charge ₹3,500.
- MRI: charge ₹6,500. Allow 45 to 60 minutes for the visit.
- Booking is open from 9 am to 5 pm. Appointments for the next day are accepted until 5 pm.

## Preparation and documents
- Bring the doctor's written request, photo ID and any previous scans.
- Remove jewellery, metal objects and hearing aids before CT and MRI.
- Tell the staff about any implant or pacemaker at booking; the radiology team will confirm whether the scan can be arranged.
- Follow the written preparation instructions given at booking, such as fasting or a full bladder for some scans.

## Reports
- Ultrasound and CT: ready in 24 hours.
- MRI: ready in 48 hours.
- Collect from the Radiology counter between 9 am and 7 pm or download from the patient portal.

## Cancellations
Cancel at least 4 hours ahead for a full refund. See the refunds document.
