## Where and when
The Health Checkup centre is on the 3rd floor of the OPD Block, ext. 2220. It is open Monday to Saturday from 7:30 am to 2 pm. Registration closes at 11 am. The checkup is closed on Sundays and public holidays.

## Packages
| Package | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | ₹2,500 | Blood and urine panels, chest X-ray, ECG, physician consultation |
| Executive | ₹5,500 | Basic plus ultrasound of the abdomen, eye check, dental check, diet consultation |
| Senior citizen (60 and above) | ₹4,000 | Basic plus bone density scan and eye check |
| Women's wellness | ₹4,500 | Basic plus breast screening and gynecology consultation |

## Booking steps
1. Call ext. 2220 between 9 am and 4 pm, or book at the counter.
2. Pay at least 50 percent in advance; the balance is paid on the day.
3. Confirm the date. Slots are limited to 30 people per day.

## Preparation
- Arrive at 7:30 am after fasting for 10 hours. Water is allowed.
- Carry photo ID and any current prescriptions.
- Wear comfortable clothes.

## What happens
Most checkups take 3 to 4 hours. A light breakfast is provided in the centre after the blood test. Reports are ready in 2 working days and a doctor discusses them with you at a follow-up slot included in the price.

## Cancellation
Cancellation up to 48 hours before gives a 90 percent refund; later cancellation gives 50 percent. See the refunds document.
