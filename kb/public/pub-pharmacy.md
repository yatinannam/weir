## Main pharmacy
The Main pharmacy is on the ground floor of the Main Block, near the Emergency entrance. It is open 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. Call ext. 2115. It serves inpatients, discharge medicines and emergency prescriptions, as well as any walk-in customers at night.

## OPD pharmacy
The OPD pharmacy is on the ground floor of the OPD Block. It is open Monday to Saturday from 8 am to 6 pm and is closed on Sundays and public holidays. Call ext. 2116. It serves patients leaving outpatient clinics. On Sundays, outpatients should use the Main pharmacy.

## Summary of the two counters
- Main pharmacy: 24 hours, every day, Main Block.
- OPD pharmacy: 8 am to 6 pm, Monday to Saturday, OPD Block.

## How to buy medicines
1. Show a valid prescription from a registered doctor. Prescriptions from other hospitals are accepted if they are dated within the last 30 days.
2. Pay at the counter by cash, card or UPI.
3. Check the pack and collect the bill.

## Discounts and services
- Inpatients receive medicines on their ward bill. No separate payment is needed.
- Senior citizens aged 60 and above get a 5 percent discount on non-discounted items, on showing photo ID.
- Home delivery within 5 km of the hospital is available from the Main pharmacy for orders above ₹500, with a delivery charge of ₹50.

## Returns
Unopened and sealed packs can be returned the same day with the bill. See the refunds document.
