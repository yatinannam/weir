## Ways to give feedback
- Feedback boxes at the main reception, each ward desk, the cafeteria and the OPD Block entrance. They are opened every morning at 9 am.
- In person at Patient relations, ground floor, Main Block, ext. 2120, Monday to Saturday from 9 am to 5 pm.
- The feedback form handed to every inpatient at discharge.
- A QR code poster on every ward, which opens the hospital's feedback form.

## Making a complaint
1. Speak first to the nurse in charge or department head. Many concerns are solved on the spot.
2. If it is not solved, write the complaint on the grievance form available at Patient relations or at any ward desk.
3. You get an acknowledgement slip with a reference number immediately.
4. The complaint is logged and sent to the department head the same day.

## Response times
- Acknowledgement: immediate.
- First response: within 48 hours.
- Final resolution: within 7 working days.
- If you are not satisfied, you can escalate to the Medical Superintendent's office on the 2nd floor of the Main Block. The office replies within 5 working days.

## Serious concerns
Complaints involving patient safety are sent to the Quality department on the same day. Confidentiality is maintained, and a complaint will never affect the care you receive.

## Compliments
Positive comments are shared with staff each month. A Patient Care Star board in the main lobby names the staff members appreciated by patients every month.

## After hours
At night, tell the nurse in charge of the ward or the Emergency desk on ext. 2100. The matter is passed to Patient relations the next morning.
