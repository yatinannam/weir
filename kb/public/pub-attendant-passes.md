## Who gets a pass
Each admitted patient is allowed one attendant, who may stay with the patient through the day and night. The attendant needs an attendant pass. Visitors do not need an attendant pass; they use visitor slips, described in the visiting hours documents.

## How to get a pass
1. At admission, the Admission desk (ext. 2105) issues one attendant pass.
2. Show the attendant's photo ID. The attendant's name is written on the pass.
3. Pay a refundable deposit of ₹50.
4. Wear the pass visibly at all times inside the wards.

## Colours
- Blue: general ward and semi-private room
- Gold: private room
- Red: ICU attendant (waiting area only, no overnight stay inside the ICU)
- Green: pediatric ward parent or guardian
- Pink: maternity birth companion

## Changing the attendant
A pass can be transferred to another family member. Both the old and the new attendant should come to the Admission desk with photo IDs. The change takes 5 minutes and costs nothing. Only one attendant may be registered at a time.

## Lost passes
A lost pass costs ₹100 to replace and the earlier deposit is forfeited. Report it at once to security on ext. 2400.

## Returning the pass
Return the pass at the Admission desk at discharge to get your ₹50 back. Refunds are made in cash.

## Rules for attendants
- Attendants may stay overnight, but must leave the ward during doctors' rounds if asked.
- Attendants get a 50 percent concession on daily parking.
- The Cafeteria sells attendant meal coupons.
