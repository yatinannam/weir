## Where and when
The Admission desk is on the ground floor of the Main Block, open 24 hours. Call ext. 2105. Planned admissions are processed between 8 am and 8 pm; at other hours, go through the Emergency department.

## Documents needed
- Admission note or letter from the treating doctor
- Photo ID of the patient and of the person completing admission
- Address proof
- Insurance card and policy details (for cashless admission)
- Scheme card and referral letter (for government scheme admission)

## Steps
1. Present your documents at the Admission desk.
2. Choose a room category: general ward, semi-private or private. See the room tariffs document.
3. Pay the admission deposit at the Billing counter and keep the receipt.
4. Receive a wristband and a ward allotment slip.
5. A porter escorts you to the ward. Check-in to the ward is possible from 7 am to 9 pm.

## Deposits
- Self-pay admission: ₹25,000 deposit.
- Cashless insurance admission: ₹5,000 deposit, adjusted once insurer approval is received.
- Government scheme admission: no deposit.

## Attendants
One attendant pass is issued per patient at admission; see the attendant passes document.

## Tips
Bring a small bag, toiletries and one set of day clothes. Leave jewellery and cash at home; the hospital provides a lockable bedside cupboard but cannot be responsible for valuables. If a bed in your chosen category is not free, you will be waitlisted and called on ext. 2105 as soon as one opens.
