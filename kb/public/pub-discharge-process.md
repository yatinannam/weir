## Discharge time
The standard discharge time is 11 am. Patients who are cleared after that time are still required to leave the room by 2 pm for room turnover, and can wait in the discharge lounge on the 4th floor of the Main Block.

## Steps
1. The doctor writes the discharge order, usually during morning rounds.
2. The ward nurse informs the billing desk. The final bill is prepared within 2 hours.
3. Settle the bill at the Billing counter, ground floor, Main Block (ext. 2110). Insurance patients pay only the non-payable items and any co-payment, after the insurer approves.
4. Collect the discharge summary, medicine list and follow-up appointment slip from the ward desk.
5. Collect any pending medicines from the Main pharmacy if needed.
6. Return the wristband and attendant pass to get the pass deposit back.

## Documents you receive
- Discharge summary (two copies)
- Final bill and receipts
- Follow-up appointment slip
- Original reports and films

## Insurance cases
For cashless patients, the final bill goes to the insurer's desk (ext. 2111) for approval, which usually takes 3 to 4 hours. Plan for a late-afternoon discharge.

## Transport
A wheelchair and porter are free of charge. The hospital ambulance for a transfer home costs ₹500 within 10 km; book on ext. 2101.

## Records
If you need extra copies of the summary later, apply at Medical Records (ext. 2210).
