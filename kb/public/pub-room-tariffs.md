## Daily tariff
All rates are per 24-hour day, from 11 am to 11 am. Nursing care, bed linen, and three meals for the patient are included.

| Room type | Floor | Rate per day |
|---|---|---|
| General ward bed (8 beds per ward) | Main Block, 4th | ₹1,200 |
| Semi-private room (2 beds) | Main Block, 5th | ₹3,500 |
| Private room (1 bed, attached bathroom) | Main Block, 6th | ₹6,000 |
| ICU bed | Main Block, 3rd | ₹9,000 |

## Semi-private versus private
A semi-private room has two beds with a curtain divider, shared bathroom and one attendant chair per bed. A private room has a single bed, its own bathroom, a television and a sofa-cum-bed for one attendant. The private room costs ₹2,500 more per day than semi-private.

## Extras
- Extra attendant bed in a private room: ₹300 per night
- Doctor visit charges are billed separately per visit
- Procedure charges and medicines are not included in the room rate

## Rules
- Day of admission and day of discharge are charged as one day if the stay is under 24 hours.
- A patient who moves between categories is charged each category at its own rate.
- Room rates are the same on weekends and holidays.

## Deposit
Deposit amounts depend on how you pay, not the room type. See the admission process, self-pay billing and insurance billing documents.
