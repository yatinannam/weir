## Visiting windows
The pediatric ward is on the 1st floor of the Mother and Child Wing. Visiting hours are daily from 10 am to 12 noon and from 5 pm to 7 pm, on weekdays and weekends alike.

## Parents and guardians
One parent or guardian may stay with the child at all times. This person receives a green attendant pass at the ward desk and may sleep on the fold-out chair beside the bed. A second parent may join during visiting windows.

## Other visitors
- Grandparents and siblings may visit only during the windows above.
- Siblings must be accompanied by an adult and must be at least 5 years old.
- A maximum of three people may be at a bedside at once.
- Visitors must sanitise hands at the ward entrance.

## Rules for the ward
- Toys and books are welcome if they are washable. The play area on the same floor is open from 3 pm to 6 pm for children who are able to go there.
- Outside food for the child must be cleared with the ward nurse.
- Photography of other patients is not allowed.
- Mobile phones should be on silent.

## Contact
Pediatric ward desk: ext. 2171. For the pediatric outpatient clinic, see the pediatrics OPD document. For adult wards, see the general ward visiting hours, which are different.
