## About the scheme
Weir General Hospital is empanelled under the Weir State Health Assurance Scheme (WSHAS). Eligible families can receive covered inpatient care without paying an admission deposit.

## Scheme desk
The WSHAS desk is on the ground floor of the Main Block next to the Admission desk, ext. 2112. It is open Monday to Saturday from 9 am to 5 pm. Outside these hours, emergency patients are admitted first and verified the next working morning.

## Documents required
- WSHAS family card
- Photo ID of the patient
- Referral letter from a primary health centre (not needed in emergencies)
- Doctor's admission note

## Steps
1. Show the card and ID at the WSHAS desk.
2. Staff verify eligibility on the scheme portal. This takes about 30 minutes.
3. A pre-authorisation request is sent for the planned treatment package.
4. After approval, the patient is admitted to a general ward bed. No deposit is collected.

## What is covered
The scheme covers the general ward bed, listed procedures, standard medicines, and diagnostic tests in the approved package. It does not cover semi-private or private rooms. If you choose a higher room, you pay the difference in daily tariff, which is ₹2,300 per day for semi-private over general ward and ₹4,800 for private, and a deposit of ₹5,000 is collected for this difference.

## Not covered
Items outside the package, cosmetic procedures, and treatments not on the scheme list are billed to the patient.

## Help
Call ext. 2112 for questions about eligibility.
