## Unused admission deposit
Any part of the admission deposit not used in the final bill is refunded at the Billing counter (ext. 2110) at the time of discharge. Refunds are made to the original mode of payment: cash deposits above ₹10,000 are refunded by bank transfer, others in cash.

## Refund timelines
- Cash refunds below ₹10,000: same day at the counter.
- Card and UPI refunds: within 7 working days to the same card or UPI account.
- Bank transfer refunds: within 5 working days. Bring a cancelled cheque leaf or a printout of your account details.

## OPD fees
- If you cancel an OPD appointment at least 24 hours before the slot by calling the department extension, the full consultation fee is refunded.
- If you cancel within 24 hours, 50 percent is refunded.
- If a doctor is unavailable on the day, you can reschedule free of charge or take a full refund.

## Tests and packages
- A booked lab test or radiology scan can be cancelled up to 2 hours before the slot for a full refund.
- Health checkup packages can be cancelled up to 48 hours before the date for a refund of 90 percent. Within 48 hours, 50 percent is refunded.

## Not refundable
Consultation fees after the consultation, medicines after leaving the pharmacy counter (unless sealed and returned the same day with the bill), and registration fees are not refundable.

## How to apply
Bring the original receipt and photo ID to the Billing counter. Disputes are handled by Patient relations on ext. 2120.
