## Days and times
The Pediatrics outpatient clinic is open Monday to Saturday from 9 am to 5 pm, with a lunch break from 1 pm to 2 pm during which only tokens already issued are seen. It is closed on Sundays and public holidays.

## Location
Mother and Child Wing, 1st floor, Room 104. The Pediatrics OPD desk is on ext. 2170. The pediatric ward is on the same floor. Strollers can be parked free of charge at the wing entrance.

## Fees
- First consultation: ₹400
- Follow-up within 15 days: ₹200
- Newborn first visits (up to 28 days old) are charged at ₹300.

## Booking
1. Register at the Mother and Child Wing ground floor counter from 8:30 am to 4:30 pm.
2. Or call ext. 2170 between 9 am and 4 pm to reserve a slot.
3. Up to 100 tokens are issued per day.

## What to bring
- The child's birth certificate or any photo ID of a parent
- The child's immunisation card
- Earlier prescriptions and reports
- A feeding bottle or snacks for waiting time

## Good to know
A baby care room with a changing table is on the same floor. The vaccination clinic for children runs on separate days; see the vaccination clinic document. A parent or guardian aged 18 or above must accompany each child.
