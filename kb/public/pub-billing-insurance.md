## Admission deposit
Patients who use cashless insurance pay only a ₹5,000 deposit at admission. The deposit is refunded or adjusted once the insurer sends final approval. This is much lower than the self-pay deposit of ₹25,000.

## Insurance desk
The Insurance and TPA desk is on the ground floor of the Main Block next to Billing, ext. 2111. It is open from 8 am to 8 pm on all days.

## Steps for cashless admission
1. Show your insurance card and photo ID at the Insurance desk before or at admission.
2. The desk sends a pre-authorisation request to the insurer. Planned admissions should send this at least 2 working days before the admission date.
3. The insurer usually replies within 4 hours for emergencies and one working day for planned admissions.
4. Once approved, the insurer pays the approved amount directly to the hospital.
5. You pay any co-payment and non-payable items at discharge.

## Non-payable items
Items commonly not covered by insurers include registration fees, personal toiletries, attendant meals, and consumables on the insurer's exclusion list. The desk gives you the list of items at admission.

## If cashless is declined
If the insurer declines or reduces the approval, you may convert the stay to self-pay. The deposit then becomes ₹25,000, with the ₹5,000 already paid counted towards it. Claims can then be filed for reimbursement; the desk provides the hospital's final bill and claim documents.

## Documents
Bring the policy card, photo ID, policy number written down, and the doctor's admission note.
