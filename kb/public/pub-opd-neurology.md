## Days and times
The Neurology outpatient clinic runs on Tuesdays, Thursdays and Saturdays from 10 am to 2 pm. It is closed on Mondays, Wednesdays, Fridays and Sundays.

## Location
OPD Block, 1st floor, Room 118. The Neurology OPD desk is on ext. 2150. It is on the same floor as the Cardiology OPD, but the two clinics run on different days.

## Fees
- First consultation: ₹700
- Follow-up within 15 days with the same doctor: ₹350
- Nerve conduction and EEG tests are booked and billed separately at the OPD Block counter.

## Booking
1. Register at the OPD Block ground floor counter between 9 am and 1:30 pm on a clinic day.
2. Or reserve a slot by calling ext. 2150 between 9 am and 4 pm on any working day.
3. A maximum of 45 tokens are issued per clinic day.

## What to bring
- Photo ID
- All earlier scan films or CDs and written reports
- A list of your current medicines as written on the prescription
- A family member or attendant if you need help moving around

## Good to know
Please arrive 20 minutes early. Wheelchairs are available at the OPD Block entrance at no charge. Patients who cannot climb stairs can use the lift on the east side of the OPD Block. If a clinic day falls on a public holiday, the clinic is held on the next working day and the notice board at the OPD entrance will say so.
