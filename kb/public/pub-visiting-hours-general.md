## Weekdays
Visitors to the general wards (Main Block, 4th floor) are welcome from Monday to Friday between 4 pm and 7 pm. Visiting is not allowed outside this window except for the registered attendant of the patient.

## Weekends and public holidays
On Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays there are two visiting windows: 11 am to 1 pm and 4 pm to 7 pm. The extra morning window lets working families visit.

## Rules for visitors
- A maximum of two visitors per patient at a time.
- Children under 12 are not allowed inside general wards.
- Every visitor must collect a visitor slip at the Main Block ground floor reception and show it at the ward door. The slip is free.
- Keep mobile phones on silent and speak softly.
- Outside food is not allowed. Fruit and sealed biscuits may be handed over after checking with the ward nurse.
- Visitors with a cough or fever should postpone the visit and call the ward instead.

## Other wards
These hours apply only to the general wards. The intensive care unit, the pediatric ward and the maternity ward have their own, different timings. See the ICU, pediatric and maternity visiting hour documents. Semi-private and private rooms on the 5th and 6th floors follow the general ward timings, but private room patients may nominate one extra visitor.

## Questions
Call the general ward help line on ext. 2135 or the main reception on ext. 2000.
