## Hours and location
The Emergency department is open 24 hours a day, every day of the year. It is on the ground floor of the Main Block, reached through Gate 3 on Riverside Road. The department desk is on ext. 2100 and the ambulance dispatch desk is on ext. 2101.

## What happens on arrival
1. A triage nurse meets every patient at the entrance and assigns a colour: red (immediate), orange (within 10 minutes), yellow (within 30 minutes) or green (within 60 minutes).
2. Treatment starts first. Registration and paperwork follow once the patient is stable.
3. A relative completes registration at the Emergency desk with a photo ID.

## Payments and deposits
- No deposit is asked before emergency treatment begins.
- If the patient is later admitted, the normal admission deposit applies. See the admission process document.
- Emergency consultation charge: ₹800, billed at the Emergency desk.

## Ambulance
The hospital runs four ambulances. Call ext. 2101 from any hospital phone. Ambulance trips within 10 km of the hospital cost ₹500, and ₹30 per additional kilometre.

## Visitors
Only one attendant per patient may stay in the Emergency bay. Other relatives wait in the family lounge next to the Emergency desk, which has seating for 40.

## Parking
Gate 3 is kept clear for ambulances. Private vehicles should use Gate 2 and walk through the ground floor walkway. A free 30-minute drop-off is available at Gate 3 for stretcher cases.
