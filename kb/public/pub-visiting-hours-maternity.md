## Visiting windows
The maternity ward is on the 2nd floor of the Mother and Child Wing. Visiting is allowed every day from 5 pm to 7 pm. In addition, the new mother's husband or partner may visit from 10 am to 12 noon.

## Who can visit
- Two visitors per mother at a time during the evening window.
- Children under 8 years are not permitted on the ward, except the mother's own older children for a short visit during the evening window.
- Visitors with a cold, fever or any infection must not enter.

## Birth companion
One birth companion of the mother's choice may stay with her through labour and recovery. The companion collects a pink attendant pass at the 2nd floor desk. Bring a photo ID. After the baby is shifted to the postnatal room, the same companion may stay overnight on the attendant bed. The extra attendant bed costs ₹300 per night in a private room and is free in other room types.

## Visitor rules
- Wash hands at the entrance sanitiser before touching the baby.
- Flowers and balloons are not allowed.
- Photography is permitted only in the family room, not in the ward.
- Keep phones on silent.

## Contact
Maternity ward desk: ext. 2181. For labour room arrivals at night, enter through the Emergency entrance on the ground floor of the Main Block and the staff will escort you upstairs.
