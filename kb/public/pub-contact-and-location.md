## Address
Weir General Hospital, 12 Riverside Road, Weir City. The hospital is open 24 hours for emergencies. Outpatient services run during the hours listed for each department.

## Getting here
- Bus: Route 14 and Route 22 stop at Riverside Gate, a two-minute walk from Gate 1.
- Auto and taxi: drop-off is under the canopy at Gate 1 on Riverside Road.
- Own vehicle: enter at Gate 2 (Canal Lane) for the parking area. See the parking document for rates.
- Gate 3 is used only by ambulances and is kept clear at all times.

## Entrances
- Gate 1: main entrance to the Main Block and reception.
- Gate 2: OPD Block and parking.
- Gate 3: Emergency and ambulances, open 24 hours.
- Gates 1 and 2 are open from 6 am to 10 pm. After 10 pm, use Gate 3.

## Contact points
All contact is by internal extension. Call the main reception on ext. 2000 and ask to be connected.
- Main reception: ext. 2000, open 24 hours.
- Emergency: ext. 2100.
- Patient relations: ext. 2120, Monday to Saturday, 9 am to 5 pm.
- Security: ext. 2400.

## Help finding your way
Volunteers in green jackets stand at Gate 1 and Gate 2 from 8 am to 6 pm to guide patients, offer wheelchairs and show the way to departments. Wheelchairs are free of charge; collect one at the reception or Gate 2 desk. See the departments directory for floors and extensions.
