## Location
The vaccination clinics are on the ground floor of the OPD Block, ext. 2225. Adult and child clinics use separate rooms (Room G4 for adults, Room G6 for children) and run on different days.

## Adult vaccination clinic
- Days: Tuesdays and Thursdays
- Time: 10 am to 1 pm
- Open to people aged 18 and above.
- Travel vaccines are given only on Thursdays with a booking made 7 days in advance.

## Child vaccination clinic
- Days: Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays
- Time: 9 am to 12 noon
- For children from birth to 12 years.
- Newborns' first vaccines are given before discharge from the Mother and Child Wing and need no separate visit.

The clinic is closed on Saturdays and Sundays for both groups.

## What to bring
- Photo ID of the adult, or the child's immunisation card and a parent's photo ID
- Earlier vaccination records
- For travel vaccines, a copy of the travel itinerary

## Steps
1. Register at the clinic desk at least 30 minutes before closing.
2. Pay the vaccine and service charge at the counter.
3. Receive the vaccine from the nurse and wait in the observation area for 20 minutes.
4. The immunisation card is updated and stamped.

## Charges
A service charge of ₹100 applies per visit, plus the price of the vaccine, which is displayed at the counter.

## Booking
Booking is optional for routine vaccines. Call ext. 2225 between 9 am and 4 pm.
