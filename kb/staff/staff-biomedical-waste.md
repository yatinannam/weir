title: Biomedical waste segregation and handling
---

## Colour-coded bins
Every ward, clinic and laboratory has four bins. Use them as follows:
- Yellow bin: anatomical waste, soiled dressings, and items soaked in blood or body fluids.
- Red bin: contaminated plastics such as tubing, gloves, catheters and plastic syringes without needles.
- White puncture-proof container: needles, blades and sharp items. Never recap a needle.
- Blue box: glass vials and broken glassware.

General waste (paper, food packaging) goes into the green bin for regular collection.

## Handling rules
1. Segregate at the point of generation. Do not mix colours.
2. Fill bins only up to three quarters. Seal the bag when it reaches that mark.
3. Label each bag with the ward name and date.
4. Wear gloves and a mask when handling waste bags, and wash hands afterwards.
5. Never carry waste bags by hand against the body; use the closed trolley.

## Collection schedule
Housekeeping staff collect biomedical waste from wards at 7 am, 3 pm and 10 pm and take it to the storage room in the basement of the Main Block. The authorised external vendor collects from the storage room every day at 11 am. Waste must not be stored for more than 48 hours.

## Records
Each ward logs the weight of waste sent in the register each shift. The Infection Control office (ext. 2320) reviews the registers every Monday and reports monthly totals.

## Spills and injuries
- Report any needle-stick injury to the Infection Control office on ext. 2320 immediately and file an incident report within 12 hours.
- For spills, cover the area with absorbent material and call Housekeeping on ext. 2340.

## Training
All new staff attend a one-hour waste session in their first week, and refresher training happens every year.
