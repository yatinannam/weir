## Contact and hours
The IT helpdesk is in the Main Block basement, Room B3. Dial ext. 2300 from any hospital phone. Staff are available from 8 am to 8 pm on weekdays and from 9 am to 5 pm on Saturdays. After hours, a duty engineer takes only critical issues on the same extension.

## What the helpdesk handles
- Password resets and locked accounts
- Hospital information system (HIS) access and errors
- Printers, scanners and label printers
- Wi-Fi and network ports
- New user accounts and email
- Phone extension faults

## Raising a ticket
1. Call ext. 2300 or use the helpdesk page on the staff portal.
2. Give your employee ID, department, location and a short description of the problem.
3. Note the ticket number you receive.
4. You will get a message when the ticket is closed. Reply within 2 days if the problem continues.

## Response targets
- Critical (HIS down, registration blocked, patient safety affected): response within 15 minutes.
- High (department printer or workstation down): 2 hours.
- Normal (access requests, software queries): 1 working day.

## Password rules
- Passwords expire every 90 days.
- Minimum length is 10 characters with a number and symbol.
- Never share your password. The helpdesk will never ask for it.
- Password reset needs your ID badge to be shown at the helpdesk or a call-back to your department head.

## Downtime procedure
During an HIS outage, departments use the paper forms stored in each ward's downtime box. The helpdesk announces recovery on the staff portal and by call to ward desks.

## Security
Report suspicious emails to the helpdesk and do not click on links. Use only approved USB devices.
