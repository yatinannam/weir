## Wearing the badge
All employees, contract staff, interns and visiting consultants must wear their hospital ID badge on the chest, with the photo facing outward, at all times on campus. Badges must be worn on a lanyard in the colour of the employee's category:
- Blue: doctors
- Green: nurses
- Grey: administrative staff
- Orange: housekeeping and security
- Red: contract and temporary staff

## Getting a badge
1. The department head sends a request to HR (ext. 2310).
2. The new employee visits the HR office, Main Block 2nd floor, with a passport-size photo and a photo ID.
3. The badge is printed in 1 working day. There is no charge for the first badge.

## Access and use
The badge works as a door access card for staff areas, a parking pass and a canteen payment card. It must never be lent to anyone else. Misuse can lead to disciplinary action.

## Lost, damaged or stolen badges
- Report a lost badge to HR and security (ext. 2400) within 24 hours so it can be deactivated.
- A replacement costs ₹200, payable at the Billing counter. A damaged badge is replaced at ₹100 on return of the old one.
- A temporary day badge can be issued by security for ₹0 once per incident, valid for 3 days.

## Leaving the hospital
Return the badge to HR on the last working day. Final settlement is released only after the badge is returned.

## Visitors and vendors
Vendors receive a numbered visitor badge from security at Gate 1 after leaving a photo ID, which is returned when they hand back the badge.

## Staff discount
Badge holders get a 20 percent discount at the cafeteria and 10 percent at the OPD pharmacy.
