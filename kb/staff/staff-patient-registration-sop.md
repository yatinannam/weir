## Purpose and scope
This procedure covers outpatient and planned inpatient registration by front desk staff at the OPD Block ground floor and the Main Block Admission desk. It does not cover emergency registration, where treatment comes first.

## Counter timings
- OPD registration counters open at 7:30 am and close at 4:30 pm, Monday to Saturday. There are 6 counters, of which 2 are reserved for senior citizens and patients with disabilities.
- Admission desk: 24 hours, ext. 2105.

## Steps
1. Greet the patient and ask for a photo ID.
2. Search the system by name and phone number to avoid duplicate records.
3. For a new patient, create a record with name, date of birth, gender, address and a contact number for the family.
4. Print the Unique Health ID (UHID) card and give it to the patient. The UHID begins with the letters WGH.
5. Collect the consultation fee and print the receipt.
6. Issue a token and direct the patient to the department.
7. For admission, additionally verify the doctor's admission note and collect the deposit amount: ₹25,000 for self-pay, ₹5,000 for cashless insurance, none for government scheme patients.

## Data rules
- Never write down or share patient details with anyone who is not the patient or a named relative.
- Fix wrong spellings only by asking a supervisor to approve the correction.
- Do not register a minor without a parent or guardian present, except in emergencies.

## Escalation
- System down: switch to the paper registration book and enter the data within 2 hours of recovery. Inform the IT helpdesk on ext. 2300.
- Duplicate UHID found: report to the supervisor on ext. 2125 before merging.

## Audit
Supervisors review 20 records every day for completeness.
