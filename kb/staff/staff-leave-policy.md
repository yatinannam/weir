## Leave entitlements per year
All permanent staff receive, per calendar year:
- Casual leave: 12 days
- Earned leave: 18 days, credited at 1.5 days per month
- Sick leave: 10 days
- Maternity leave: 26 weeks for the first two children
- Paternity leave: 10 days, to be taken within 3 months of the birth
- Bereavement leave: 5 days for the death of a parent, spouse or child

Probationary staff receive 1 day of casual leave per month, with no earned leave until confirmation.

## How to apply
1. Submit the leave request on the staff portal at least 3 working days in advance for casual and earned leave.
2. The department head approves or declines within 2 working days.
3. HR confirms the balance on the portal.
4. For leave of more than 5 days, the head of department must arrange cover and record it on the roster.

## Sick leave
Tell your supervisor by 7 am on the day, or 1 hour before the shift. For absence of 3 or more days, submit a medical certificate to HR on return.

## Carry forward and encashment
- Up to 30 days of earned leave can be carried forward.
- Earned leave above 30 days lapses at the end of December.
- Casual and sick leave cannot be carried forward.
- Encashment is allowed only at retirement or resignation.

## Restrictions
Casual leave cannot be combined with more than 3 days of public holiday. Leave is not normally approved during the annual accreditation week.
