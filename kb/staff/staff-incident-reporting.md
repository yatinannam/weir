## What to report
Any event that harmed, or could have harmed, a patient, visitor or staff member must be reported. This includes:
- Falls and near-misses
- Wrong patient, wrong sample or wrong label events
- Equipment failures
- Needle-stick injuries and exposure to hazardous waste
- Medication events (report the event only; clinical review is handled separately)
- Security events, aggression and thefts
- Fire alarms and false alarms

## How to report
1. Make sure the person is safe and tell your supervisor immediately.
2. Fill in form IR-1 on the staff portal under "Quality". A paper copy is available at every ward desk if the system is down.
3. File the form within 12 hours of the event, before the end of your shift where possible.
4. Describe only facts: who, where, when, what happened. Do not blame individuals.
5. Note the incident number received on submission.

## What happens next
- The Quality department (ext. 2330) acknowledges the report within 1 working day.
- Serious events, such as a death or major injury, are reviewed within 72 hours by a committee chaired by the Medical Superintendent.
- Less serious events are reviewed weekly by the department head.
- The reporter gets feedback within 14 days.

## No-blame policy
Reports are used for learning and not for punishment, except in cases of wilful misconduct. Anonymous reporting is possible using the red box outside the Quality office on the Main Block 2nd floor.

## Reporting targets
Each ward is expected to submit at least 2 near-miss reports every month to keep the reporting culture active.

## Contact
For questions, call the Quality department on ext. 2330.
