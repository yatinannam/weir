## Nurse shifts
Nursing staff work three shifts in every ward:
- Morning shift: 7 am to 2 pm
- Evening shift: 2 pm to 9 pm
- Night shift: 9 pm to 7 am

Handover takes place in the last 15 minutes of every shift at the ward desk. A nurse works six days a week with one weekly off assigned by the nurse in charge.

## Doctor shifts
Resident doctors and duty medical officers work three shifts:
- Day shift: 8 am to 4 pm
- Evening shift: 4 pm to 12 midnight
- Night shift: 12 midnight to 8 am

Consultants work outpatient clinics and on-call rosters published monthly by the Medical Superintendent's office. Doctors hold a combined handover at 8 am, 4 pm and 12 midnight in the Main Block, 3rd floor conference room.

## Key differences
Nurse shifts start at 7 am, 2 pm and 9 pm. Doctor shifts start at 8 am, 4 pm and midnight. The night shift for nurses is 10 hours; for doctors it is 8 hours.

## Support staff
- Housekeeping: 6 am to 2 pm and 2 pm to 10 pm
- Security: 6 am to 2 pm, 2 pm to 10 pm and 10 pm to 6 am
- Front desk and billing: 8 am to 8 pm in two overlapping shifts (8 am to 4 pm and 12 noon to 8 pm)

## Swaps and punctuality
- Swap shifts only with the written approval of the department head, at least 24 hours in advance.
- Staff should scan their ID badge at the entrance. A grace period of 10 minutes applies, up to three times a month.
- For roster queries, call HR on ext. 2310.
