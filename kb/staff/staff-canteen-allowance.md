---

## Who is eligible
All permanent and contract employees who work a full shift are eligible for the staff canteen allowance. Interns and trainees receive a reduced rate. Staff on leave do not receive the allowance for those days.

## Allowance amounts
- Day or evening shift: ₹150 per shift worked
- Night shift: ₹200 per shift worked
- Interns and trainees: ₹100 per shift worked
- Overtime shifts of more than 4 extra hours: an additional ₹75

## How it is paid
The allowance is credited to the employee's ID badge wallet and is not paid out in cash. HR (ext. 2310) credits the amount by the 5th of each month for the shifts worked in the previous month. Any balance unused carries over for up to 3 months, after which it lapses.

## Where it can be used
- The Staff Canteen, Main Block, 1st floor, open 6 am to 11 pm, serving four meals a day.
- The public cafeteria on the ground floor, where badge holders also get a 20 percent discount.
- It cannot be used at the pharmacies or the parking booth.

## How to use
1. Tap your ID badge on the reader at the billing counter.
2. The balance is shown on the screen.
3. If the bill exceeds the balance, pay the rest by cash or UPI.

## Problems
- If the credit is missing by the 7th, send a request to HR with your employee ID and shifts list.
- Report a lost badge at once to HR and security on ext. 2400 so the wallet can be frozen. The balance is moved to the replacement badge.
- Wrong amounts will be corrected in the following month's credit.
