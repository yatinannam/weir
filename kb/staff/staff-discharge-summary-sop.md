## Purpose
This procedure ensures that every discharged patient leaves with an accurate, signed discharge summary, within the time limit, so that discharge by 11 am is possible.

## Responsibilities
- Resident doctor: drafts the summary in the hospital information system.
- Consultant: reviews and signs the summary.
- Ward nurse: checks that the demographic details and medicine list match the chart, and prints the copies.
- Ward clerk: files one copy in the record and hands one to the patient.

## Timeline
1. Discharge order is entered by the consultant, ideally by 9 am during morning rounds.
2. The resident drafts the summary within 2 hours of the order.
3. The consultant signs within 1 hour of the draft.
4. The nurse prints two copies and explains the follow-up date to the patient.
5. For planned discharges the draft should be started the previous evening.

## Required sections
- Patient name, UHID, admission and discharge dates
- Final diagnosis and procedures done, written by the doctor
- Summary of hospital stay
- Medicines on discharge, as written by the doctor
- Follow-up date and the clinic extension
- Warning signs and the Emergency contact, ext. 2100

## Quality checks
- Names and dates are cross-checked against the registration record.
- A summary may not be released without a consultant signature.
- Medical Records (ext. 2210) audits 25 summaries every week.
- Missing summaries are reported to the department head every Monday.

## Special cases
- Leaving against medical advice: the summary carries the signed consent form.
- Transfers to another hospital: a transfer summary is given in 1 hour, and the ambulance desk is called on ext. 2101.
- Deaths: the death summary is completed within 4 hours and issued through Medical Records.
